# Procurement Sciences

> Procurement Sciences is an AI platform for government contractors that automates the full capture-to-contract lifecycle — opportunity search and bid/no-bid analysis, competitive intelligence and capture planning, compliant proposal generation and AI-assisted drafting, and post-award contract and recompete management — across federal, state, and local markets. The company publishes no public developer API surface; this profile is enriched from its public website and security & compliance disclosures.

## Platform

- [Platform](https://www.procurementsciences.com/platform): AI across the government-contracting lifecycle — opportunity search, capture, proposals, and contract management.
- [Security & Compliance](https://www.procurementsciences.com/platform/security): SOC 2 Type II, CMMC Level 2, FedRAMP Moderate, NIST 800-171, and FIPS 140-2 posture.
- [Trust Portal](https://trust.procurementsciences.com/): Certifications and security artifacts available on request.
- [Request Pricing](https://www.procurementsciences.com/request-pricing): Pricing request form.
- [ROI Calculator](https://www.procurementsciences.com/roi-calculator): Model the return on adopting the platform.
